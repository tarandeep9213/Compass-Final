# Alarm Tester User Guide

As an **Alarm Tester**, you perform the monthly alarm tests and the semi-annual cellular + camera backup checks for your assigned buildings.

## What you can do

- Run **monthly alarm tests** covering every zone in the building.
- Record zone results: **Tested**, **Not Tested**, or **Issue Found** (with notes).
- Complete **biannual checks** (cellular backup and camera backup).
- **Upload** security reports and biannual evidence documents (PDF, Excel, image).
- Save work as a **Draft** and resume later.
- Resubmit after an Approver's feedback.

## Getting started

1. Open **My Tests** to see your assigned buildings.
2. Start the monthly test, walk through each zone, mark the result.
3. When the biannual window opens, complete the cellular + camera sections.
4. Upload your evidence before submitting for approval.

## Tips

- Draft auto-save keeps your work across sessions.
- Every rejection reason is preserved — you can see the history of feedback.
- Attachment types supported: PDF, Excel, image. Size caps apply.
