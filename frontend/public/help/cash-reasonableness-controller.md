# Cash Reasonableness Test — Controller Guide

The **Cash Reasonableness Test** is a Controller-side tool that evaluates whether an operator's reported cash count is plausible against historical trends before you approve it.

## What you can do

- Run a reasonableness test for any recent operator submission.
- See **expected ranges** for each section (Currency, Rolled Coin, Counting Machines, etc.) based on the last 30 days.
- Flag submissions where one or more sections fall **outside expected range**.
- Attach a reasonableness decision to your approval or rejection.

## Getting started

1. Open **Cash Reasonableness Test** from the sidebar.
2. Pick a location and date.
3. The system shows the operator's reported totals next to the expected min/max per section.
4. Sections outside the band are highlighted amber (warning) or red (significant).
5. Add your notes and choose an action: **Accept**, **Query operator**, or **Reject with reasoning**.

## Tips

- The reasonableness bands use a rolling window — noisy outliers settle out as history grows.
- Use the **Query operator** option for ambiguous cases; it triggers an in-system message rather than a hard rejection.
- Your reasonableness decision is captured in the audit trail with the full context.
