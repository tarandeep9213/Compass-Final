# Alarm Approver User Guide

As an **Alarm Approver**, you review submitted alarm tests and biannual checks, approve what's complete, and provide clear feedback for anything that isn't.

## What you can do

- Review **pending alarm tests** and biannual checks for your assigned buildings.
- **Approve** complete, well-evidenced submissions.
- **Reject** with a reason — the tester sees your note and can resubmit.
- Monitor **Escalations** — Tier 1/2/3 overdue buildings.
- View **Compliance** and **Trends** dashboards across buildings.
- Access the **Audit Trail** for full history.

## Getting started

1. Open **Pending Approvals** to see what's in your queue.
2. Click Review to inspect zone results, biannual checks, attachments.
3. Approve or Reject. If you reject, a clear reason helps the tester fix it fast.
4. Watch the **Escalations** screen for buildings that are falling behind.

## Tips

- Rejection reasons are preserved in the audit trail — cycle-by-cycle.
- An Approver action does not bypass a missing biannual check — require evidence.
- Tier 3 escalation loops in the Regional Controller (and DGM for biannual).
